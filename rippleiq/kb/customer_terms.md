# Helix Robotics — Customer Delivery Terms

Late-delivery penalties and delay-notification duties per customer. Source: Sales Operations.

## Toyota Tsusho Logistics
- Account tier: Strategic account
- Late-delivery penalty: 2.0% of order value per week, capped at 10%
- Delay notification required: at least 7 days before the expected delay
- Clause: Late delivery incurs 2.0% of order value per week, capped at 10%. Helix must notify Toyota Tsusho Logistics at least 7 days before any expected delay; force majeure relief applies only if notice is given.
