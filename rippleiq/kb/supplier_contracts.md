# Helix Robotics — Supplier Contract Register

Key contractual terms for every direct-material supplier. Source: Legal & Procurement.

## S01 — Formosa Semicon (Hsinchu, Taiwan)
- Reliability tier: A
- Primary supplier for: P01 Motion control MCU, P02 Motor driver IC, P18 Vision SoC
- Qualified alternate for: none
- Force majeure clause: Formosa Semicon shall notify Helix within 3 days of any force majeure event and provide a written recovery plan within 10 days. During shortage, allocation to Helix shall be no less than pro-rata share of pre-event volume.
- Allocation guarantee during shortage: pro-rata
- Expedite cost sharing: 50/50 split on air freight
- Business continuity plan on file: yes
- Backup production site: none
